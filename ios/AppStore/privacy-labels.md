# App Privacy Answers

Declare all items below as **Linked to the User**, **Not Used for Tracking**, and primarily **App Functionality** unless App Store Connect asks otherwise.

- Contact Info: Name, Email Address
- Location: Precise Location
- Sensitive Info: ethnicity and dating/profile preferences supplied by the user
- User Content: Photos or Videos, Emails or Text Messages, Other User Content, Customer Support
- Identifiers: User ID
- Purchases: Purchase History
- Financial Info: Other Financial Info, only if the optional profile income field remains in production

The app does not use advertising identifiers, third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, contacts, microphone, health data, or payment-card data. Apple and RevenueCat process subscription payments; NYX does not receive card details.

Before submission, compare these answers with Xcode's generated Privacy Report from the archive and include data declared by every bundled third-party SDK.
